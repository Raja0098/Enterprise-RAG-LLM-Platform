Family includes.— A person who seeks benefit of reservation,\n   his/her parents and siblings below the age of 18 years as also his/her spouse\n   and children below the age of 18 years.\n\n5. Certificate.— The benefit of reservation under EWS can be availed\n   upon production of an Income and Asset Certificate issued by the prescribed\n   Authority.
